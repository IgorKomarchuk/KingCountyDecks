Do you offer window installation in King County?

Yes. King County Decks lists window installation and replacement as current services. Contact us to discuss your project, materials and location.

Can a foggy window be repaired instead of replaced?

It depends on where the condensation forms, the insulated glass seal and the condition of the frame. We can discuss options and confirm whether glass repair is available for your request.

Which cities do you serve?

The company lists Seattle, Bellevue, Tacoma, Redmond, Federal Way, Kent, Renton, Auburn, Kirkland and surrounding communities. See our service areas page for details.

How do I request a quote?

Use the website form or call 206.944.2857. Share a description, approximate measurements and photos where possible for a useful first conversation.
