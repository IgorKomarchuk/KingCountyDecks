King County Decks is a Washington-based exterior construction company serving homeowners across King County and the Greater Seattle area.

Founded by Max Kovalev and Sergey Kuyan, the business draws on more than 23 years of residential building experience through MJK Construction. Its publicly listed services include window installation and replacement, alongside decks, siding and roofing.

This window-focused website gives customers one place to explore installations, discuss possible repair needs and reach the same local team.
